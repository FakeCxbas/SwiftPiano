Source distribution: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander

This application bundles the 30 Ogg Vorbis reference notes from the Tone.js
distribution, spanning A0–C8 at minor-third intervals. These are a reduced
single-layer subset, not the full 16-velocity-layer SFZ instrument. Ogg files
are redistributed unmodified. Runtime playback transposes neighboring notes
by at most one semitone and applies gain, release envelopes and a bus limiter.
